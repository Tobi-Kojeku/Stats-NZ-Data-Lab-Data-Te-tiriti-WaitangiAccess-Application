# SAFE 2 — Legal 

## 2.1 Lawful purpose and approved use
We will use Stats NZ Data Lab microdata only for the approved research purpose and in accordance with:
- all Stats NZ Data Lab conditions,
- confidentiality requirements,
- data access agreements and any approved amendments.

## 2.2 Treaty of Waitangi / human rights responsiveness
We will ensure Treaty-aligned and human-rights-responsive conduct through **Ngā Tikanga Paihere**, including:
- culturally safe interpretation,
- meaningful engagement practices where appropriate,
- accountability and responsiveness in how input informs interpretation and communication.

## 2.3 Governance compliance
The project organisation will maintain internal governance processes to ensure:
- only authorised staff can access and work with approved outputs,
- use remains consistent with the approved scope,
- compliance checks occur throughout the project lifecycle.

## 2.4 Auditability
We will keep records sufficient to demonstrate compliance with project conditions, including documentation connected to outputs and the responsiveness log used for interpretation decisions.
