# Objectives and Method Overview

## Objectives
1. **Evaluation (last 10 years):**  
   Estimate relationships over time between migration conditions and childbirth-related outcomes.

2. **Linked-cohort / time-related analysis (where feasible):**  
   Use linkage-capable datasets to support cohort and timing analyses connecting migration/residency indicators to later childbirth outcomes, subject to Stats NZ Data Lab-approved linkage structure and governance constraints.

3. **Scenario forecasting:**  
   Produce scenario-based forecasts for childbirth-related indicators and population growth under multiple plausible migration scenarios (generic definitions dependent on available Data Lab variables).

4. **Interpretation and communication:**  
   Interpret findings responsibly and communicate results clearly, including uncertainty and limitations, using **Ngā Tikanga Paihere** principles to support culturally safe, accountable interpretation.

## High-level method steps
> Note: This is a mock method overview; final methods depend on approved variables, linkage capability, and disclosure rules.

### Step 1 — Define outcomes (childbirth-related)
- Construct childbirth-related outcome metrics appropriate for the approved dataset(s).
- Use approved denominators for rate construction.

### Step 2 — Define migration/cohort constructs (generic)
- Use **generic migration/residency indicators** that are available in Stats NZ Data Lab outputs to define:
  - migrant cohorts and/or
  - timing windows relevant to the research question.
- Apply Data Lab-approved rules for variable construction and cohort selection.

### Step 3 — Evaluation analysis (historical relationships)
- Model associations between migration conditions/cohort definitions and childbirth outcomes over the **last 10 years**.
- Conduct sensitivity checks consistent with approved analysis plans.

### Step 4 — Scenario forecasting
- Combine evaluation insights with demographic structure effects to produce forecasts under multiple migration scenarios.
- Keep scenario assumptions explicit and communicate uncertainty.

### Step 5 — Disclosure-safe outputs
- Generate only outputs allowed by Stats NZ Data Lab disclosure controls (aggregation, suppression/rounding where required).
- Follow internal review before any external communication.

## Deliverables 
- Evaluation summary (methods + results interpretation)
- Scenario forecast outputs (disclosure-safe)
- Documentation of governance and responsiveness log entries linked to interpretation decisions
