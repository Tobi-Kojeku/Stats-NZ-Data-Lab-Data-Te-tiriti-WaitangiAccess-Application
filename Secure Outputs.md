# SAFE 4 — Secure Outputs

## 4.1 Export controls
We will export only outputs permitted by Stats NZ Data Lab rules and approved workflows, including:
- compliant aggregation levels,
- suppression/rounding where required.

## 4.2 Output review and approval
All outputs intended for external sharing or publication will:
- undergo internal review,
- follow Stats NZ Data Lab output approval processes.

## 4.3 Responsible communication
We will communicate results responsibly to minimise harm and misinterpretation by:
- stating assumptions and uncertainty clearly,
- providing context and limitations,
- avoiding deficit or discriminatory framing,
- ensuring that small-group inference risks are considered.

## 4.4 Output documentation
We will maintain documentation showing:
- what was produced,
- the disclosure treatment applied,
- the approval steps taken prior to release.
