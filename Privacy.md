# SAFE 3 — Privacy

## 3.1 Data minimisation
We will request and use only the variables needed to meet the research objectives, including:
- only minimum necessary detail for linkage/cohort definition consistent with Stats NZ Data Lab approval,
- no extraneous personal identifiers.

## 3.2 Confidentiality and non-disclosure
- Analysis will be conducted only within the Stats NZ Data Lab secure environment.
- We will not attempt to identify individuals.
- We will apply required disclosure avoidance techniques for any outputs.

## 3.3 Intermediate outputs and derived variables
Derived variables and intermediate outputs will be handled according to Stats NZ Data Lab rules, including:
- secure storage requirements,
- retention and deletion rules where applicable,
- restricted access consistent with approved project permissions.

## 3.4 Risk assessment 
This project will follow Stats NZ Data Lab processes for assessing privacy risk, including reviewing:
- small group/low-count scenarios,
- outputs that may be vulnerable to re-identification through inference,
- aggregation levels and disclosure thresholds prior to release.
