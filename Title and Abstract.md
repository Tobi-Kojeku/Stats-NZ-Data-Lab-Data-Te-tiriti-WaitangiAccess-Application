# Title and Abstract

## Project title
**Migration and Childbirth Outcomes in Aotearoa New Zealand: Evaluation and Scenario Forecasting Using Linked Microdata**

## Abstract
This mock application proposes an evaluation and scenario-forecasting study using linked microdata accessed through the Stats NZ Data Lab environment. The research aims to examine how migration over the last 10 years is associated with childbirth-related outcomes and population growth in Aotearoa New Zealand.

The study uses a two-part design: (1) evaluation, analysing historical relationships over time between migration conditions and childbirth outcomes, and (2) scenario forecasting, producing forward-looking estimates under multiple plausible migration scenarios combined with demographic structure effects.

The project is written from a Research Institute perspective and emphasises responsible governance, confidentiality protections, and Treaty-aligned, culturally safe interpretation through Ngā Tikanga Paihere. Ethnicity/privacy-sensitive detail is kept minimal in the mock write-up; final analytic approach depends on Stats NZ Data Lab approved variables and disclosure constraints.

## Coverage 
- Geography: **New Zealand (both North and South Islands)**
- Time horizon: **last 10 years**
