# Ngā Tikanga Paihere  — Aotearoa / Treaty-Responsive Interpretation & Accountability

> Purpose: Provide a mock, portfolio Ngā Tikanga Paihere section showing how culturally safe engagement and responsiveness will be incorporated into interpretation and communication of Results derived from Stats NZ Data Lab microdata.

---

## A) Identifying suitable relationships
The organisation will identify and where needed) establish suitable partnership relationships with people/groups who can support culturally safe and ethical interpretation relevant to:
- communities affected by migration and whānau impacts, and
- childbirth-related and population implications.

Suitable relationships may include:
- Māori advisory or capability expertise for culturally appropriate interpretation,
- community or expert advisors relevant to migration impacts and whānau wellbeing,
- subject-matter experts to support responsible demographic interpretation.

---

## B) Balancing relationships and bias in the research
Engagement will be used to ensure participation adds value by:
- informing interpretation so that results are not taken out of context,
- identifying potential blind spots or stereotypical framing,
- supporting careful communication of uncertainty and limitations,
- reducing the risk of “deficit” narratives about migrant communities or families.

---

## C) Finding the right people to consult
Consulted people/groups will be selected based on:
- authority and/or expertise relevant to the research topic,
- culturally safe practice and capability,
- lived experience or community perspectives relevant to interpretation and communication.

Consultation will focus on culturally safe framing, interpretation considerations, and how findings could be communicated responsibly.

---

## D) Building relationships early
We recognise that building suitable relationships takes time. The organisation will:
- start engagement early (before finalising interpretation and communication approaches),
- schedule milestone check-ins before key steps (e.g., choice of interpretation framing, uncertainty messaging, and any outputs intended for external audiences).

---

## E) Public interest and community benefit
The research is intended to create public value by improving understanding of how migration is associated with childbirth outcomes and population growth—supporting evidence-based planning and evaluation for New Zealand communities and stakeholders.

The organisation will aim for:
- transparency about what the analysis can and cannot conclude,
- responsible communication of insights to support informed decision-making.

---

## F) Showing evidence of accountability
Accountability will be demonstrated by:
- ensuring engagement participants understand the project purpose, outputs, and intended use,
- ensuring meaningful engagement occurs at relevant points in the project lifecycle,
- documenting how input influenced interpretation and communication decisions.

We will maintain a Responsiveness Log to record:
- feedback received,
- decisions made,
- rationale where feedback is not adopted,
- what changes (if any) were made to analysis interpretation and output framing.

---

## G) Involving community early in project decisions
Community/advisory input will be sought early to support project decisions including:
- culturally safe interpretation framing,
- identification of potentially sensitive implications and appropriate wording,
- guidance on responsible communication of results, including discussion of limitations and uncertainty.

This early involvement helps ensure outputs are communicated in ways that are understandable, contextual, and culturally safe.

---

## Responsibilities 
- **Project sponsor / accountable lead:** ensures Ngā Tikanga Paihere processes are followed and documented.
- **Data governance lead:** ensures compliance with Stats NZ Data Lab conditions.
- **Analyst(s):** documents interpretation decisions and supports responsiveness log updates.
- **Advisors / advisors (where included):** provide culturally safe guidance on interpretation and communication.
