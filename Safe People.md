# SAFE 5 — Safe People

## 5.1 Training and capability
Project team members will complete required microdata and platform training before accessing or working with Stats NZ Data Lab datasets. This includes any pathway requirements such as the **Ministry of Health online course** if required by the access approach.

Training completion will be recorded internally.

## 5.2 Organisational governance
As this is a Research Institute submission, we will define:
- project sponsor / accountable lead,
- data governance lead,
- day-to-day analyst responsibilities,
- review roles for output approval and compliance checking.

## 5.3 Cultural and ethical competence - Ngā Tikanga Paihere 
We will include appropriate advisory or capability-building to strengthen culturally safe and Treaty-aligned interpretation and communication, consistent with **Ngā Tikanga Paihere**.

## 5.4 Accountability and documentation
Team members will:
- follow approved analysis plans,
- document key decisions affecting interpretation,
- maintain a responsiveness log that records how engagement informs decisions (including rationale where feedback is not adopted).
